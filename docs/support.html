Frequently asked questions

Does Honeycomb require an account or internet connection?
No. Honeycomb runs entirely offline and never requires sign-in. The only network request it ever makes is a manual, user-initiated "Check for Updates" action.

Does Honeycomb have in-app purchases or ads?
No. All games and customization options are fully unlocked on install, with no ads and no paywalls.

Where is my data stored?
All progress, statistics, and customizations are stored locally on your device only. See the Privacy Policy for details.

I found a bug. What should I include in my report?
Please email a description of what happened, the steps to reproduce it, your device model, and OS version to honeycomb@isabee.io.
