# Beekeeper: play-test sheet (Windows)

Thank you for testing! Beekeeper is meant to feel slow and calm. Nothing here can be lost
except by pressing New Game. Please write down anything that feels confusing, unfair,
too slow, too fast, or just odd, with roughly how long you'd been playing.

## Start
1. Open Honeycomb and pick **Beekeeper** from the game list at the top.
2. You should see a picture frame, a **Catch Bee** button, a line saying "Bare hands · 1 bee",
   a story log called **The Garden**, and **BEES 0** at the top. Nothing else yet.

## The first few minutes
3. Click **Catch Bee** a few times. Most clicks give you a bee. Sometimes a bee stings you
   (coral text, no bee).
4. After your first bee, the **Hive** card and **HONEY** appear. Honey now rises on its own.
5. When you have 10 honey, a **bear** arrives with a name. Press **Sell 10** or **Sell All** to
   trade honey for **Beebits**.
6. At 15 Beebits, the **Beekeeper's Shop** opens. Tools must be bought in order: Padded Gloves,
   then Mesh Net, then Bee-Safe Vacuum. With gloves on, bees never sting.
7. Every 50 honey makes 1 **wax**. At 50 wax, the **Combs** card appears. Building a comb lets
   the hive hold 200 more honey; each comb costs a bit more wax than the last.

## Leaving and coming back
8. Switch to another game (for example Klondike) for a minute, then come back. Honey should
   have gone up.
9. Close Honeycomb completely, wait at least 5 minutes, and open it again. The story log
   should say how much honey the bees made while you were away.
10. Let honey reach its limit (for example "200 / 200"). Honey stops there, and the log says
    "The combs are full of honey." once.

## Other things to try
11. Open **Help** from the menu and read the Beekeeper section. Does it match what you saw?
12. In **Options**, switch the language to **Español** and back. All Beekeeper words, including
    old story-log lines, should change language, and nothing should be cut off.
13. Press **New Game**. It should ask "Start over?" before throwing your hive away. (Only do
    this at the very end!)

## Please tell us
- How long until the bear arrived? The shop? Your first comb?
- Was there a stretch where you had nothing to do or nothing to look forward to?
- Did anything feel like it was rushing you?
- Any words that read oddly, in English or Spanish?
